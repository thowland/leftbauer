# Left Bauer beta

Thanks for trying Left Bauer. It is Euchre for one: you and a computer partner against two computer opponents, with no accounts and no ads, and once it is installed it plays with no signal at all.

## Installing

**iPhone or iPad (Safari):**

1. Open **https://leftbauer.beta-site.us** in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open it from the home screen, where it runs full screen like any other app.

**Android (Chrome):** open the link and tap **Install app** (or ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**).

**Computer:** play in the browser, or use the install icon in the address bar in Chrome or Edge.

Updates install themselves. After a new release, the first launch downloads it and the second one runs it, and your game in progress and your stats stay on your device throughout.

## Getting around

- **New game** starts against the opponents and level chosen in **Settings**. Your partner always plays Sharp, by the conventions described under **How your partner plays**.
- **Hint** suggests a play and gives the reason. **Last trick** shows the trick just taken.
- **Menu** holds this game's rules, How to play, Settings, Home, New game and Concede, plus the sound on a phone held upright. A game you leave mid-hand is saved, and **Continue** on the home screen picks it up.
- The house rules (stick the dealer, game to 5/7/10/11, defending alone, farmer's hand and more) are under **Settings**, and they apply from the next new game.
