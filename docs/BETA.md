# Left Bauer beta

Thanks for trying Left Bauer! It's Euchre for one: you and a computer partner against two computer opponents. There are no accounts or ads, and once it's installed it plays with no signal at all.

## Install it

**iPhone or iPad (Safari):**

1. Open **https://leftbauer.beta-site.us** in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Open it from the home screen. It runs full screen, like any other app.

**Android (Chrome):** open the link and tap **Install app** (or ⋮ → **Add to Home screen**).

**Computer:** just play in the browser, or use the install icon in Chrome's or Edge's address bar.

Updates arrive by themselves: after a new release, the second launch runs it. Your game in progress and your stats stay on your device.

## Getting around

- **New game** starts against the opponents and level chosen in **Settings**. Your partner always plays the Sharp conventions, described under **How your partner plays**.
- **Hint** suggests a play and says why. **Last trick** shows the trick just taken.
- **Menu** has the sound, this game's rules, How to play, and the way out. A game left mid-hand is saved; **Continue** picks it up.
- House rules (stick the dealer, game to 5/7/10/11, defending alone, farmer's hand and more) are under **Settings**, and apply from the next new game.

## What I'd like to know

Send whatever you notice. These help the most:

1. Did it install, and does it open from your home screen without a network? Which phone or tablet?
2. Did anything on the table jump, overlap or get cut off? A screenshot is perfect.
3. Do the computer players feel fair, at each level? Did any play look like cheating, or plain silly?
4. Is your partner useful? Did it ever do something you couldn't make sense of, even after a Hint?
5. Which house rules does your table play that are missing or work differently here?
6. Is the pace right? Too fast to follow, or too slow?
7. Anything confusing, from the first tap to the end of a game?
